About the model and guidance

The model is generated locally from your traced geometry with Three.js. It uses an orthographic camera, initially 50° above horizontal. Curves are approximated by small connected panels; windows and doorways are cut into the wall geometry. The view follows the proportions of your tracing. It has no measured scale. Wall heights and openings are visual proportions, not surveyed dimensions.

Sun position uses NOAA approximate equations and UAE time. 3D lighting and shadows respond to the sun; gold window patches are illustrative and do not measure illumination. Neighbouring buildings, balconies, glazing properties, floor elevation and weather are not included. Interior windows are excluded from exterior-window exposure counts.

Feng Shui uses a compass Bagua overview plus sourced room guidance; building period, facing and personal information would be needed for a full classical assessment. Vastu uses published room-location preferences with contextual alternatives. Room positions use the marker and each floor’s bounding-box centre. Source links appear beside the guidance. These are cultural interpretations, not scientific guarantees.
